# Historical GEp-IV proposals

Located in the Jefferson Lab public proposal archive on 2026-10-22.

| Version | Local file | Archive source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Original PAC34 submission (2009), 37 pages | [Original proposal](GEp-IV_PR12-09-001_PAC34_2009.pdf) | [JLab PDF](https://www.jlab.org/exp_prog/proposals/09/PR12-09-001.pdf) |
| PAC37 revision (2011), 45 pages | [Revised proposal](GEp-IV_PR12-09-001_PAC37_2011.pdf) | [JLab PDF](https://www.jlab.org/exp_prog/proposals/proposal_updates/PR12-09-001_PAC37.pdf) |

Both are titled GEp/GMp with an 11 GeV electron beam. The original proposed SHMS with the existing FPP and BigCal, reaching Q² = 13 GeV². The revision proposed HMS with the existing FPP and BigCal, reaching Q² = 11 GeV²; its opening rationale discusses conditional approval and complementarity with Hall A GEp-V.

The archive identifies the proposal as PR12-09-001 / C12-09-001. The revision itself also uses E12-09-101; keep this alternative identifier in mind when searching historical documents.

The original cites an earlier Letter of Intent, LOI12-06-103, presented to PAC30 in 2006. That letter has not been downloaded here. These were proposals for upgraded beam operation, submitted before upgraded operations began.

The [PAC37 report](https://www.jlab.org/exp_prog/PACpage/PAC37/PAC37_Report.pdf) lists C12-09-001 as deferred and unrated, following conditional approval at PAC34. Hall A GEp-V is the distinct E12-07-109 proposal.

The PAC37 revision received a full-document review during the initial DVCS-benchmark exploration; see the [archived findings](../archive/dvcs_benchmark_exploration_2026-10-24/findings.md) for page-indexed simulation results, assumptions, and implications for HMS+FPP+NPS. The current synthesis is the [proposal-readiness summary](../proposal_readiness_summary.md). The PAC34 original has only been checked for identification and historical scope, not reviewed in full.
